# The Warden's Relic, 26 September 2026 (M16)

Table: `lost-idol-2026-09-26.md`, 50 seeds per class, vision, relic intent, and
bot style (3,000 runs). The band tests in `src/web/src/tests/balanceBands.test.ts`
pass, with the warm-up band restated as a rate (below).

## Barry's brief

Approved from the M15 proposal: a relic on level two that saves its bearer
once, guarded by a much stronger monster, with a verse as the only clue.
Additive, so a player who leaves it alone has the vanilla game. The harness
models players who heed the verse and players who seek the relic, and the
warden's strength is tuned to taste. On the verse, Barry: "I love the verse".

## What was built

- **The relic** is a new tile. Get puts it in the pack ("A RELIC! IT WILL SAVE
  THEE ONCE"), and the status panel shows RELIC HELD. The next blow that would
  kill the bearer, from a monster or from exhaustion, instead restores strength
  and vitality to their starting values, and the relic crumbles ("THE RELIC
  CRUMBLES AND THOU LIVEST").
- **The warden** is a new monster tier: legacy code 118, power 14, 84 hit
  points. The strongest archival monster is power 9 with 54.
- **Placement.** The relic lies at the west end of level two's bottom row, in a
  niche one square deep. The warden sleeps in the niche mouth, so the relic is
  reached only through it.
- **The verse** appears on the between-level panel after level one, under
  "Words are carved above the stair down", and on the progress panel
  throughout level two:

  > In the western dark a relic lies,
  > Its warden sleeps with open eyes.
  > Take heart, and it may serve thee well,
  > Or leave it be and live to tell.

- **Heeding is safe.** In all 1,500 heeding runs (every class, vision, and
  style) the warden was never woken. The heeding bot takes the warden's exact
  square from the verse; for a player who never reads it, see below.

## Choosing the warden's strength

The sweep used careful bots seeking the relic, 40 seeds each. The heeding row is
from the 50-seed report. Win %:

| Warden power | Barbarian | Warrior | Wanderer | Cleric | Mage |
| --- | --- | --- | --- | --- | --- |
| Heeding, no map | 100 | 92 | 78 | 44 | 86 |
| 12, no map | 100 | 98 | 78 | 13 | 98 |
| 14, no map (chosen) | 100 | 93 | 50 | 8 | 90 |
| 16, no map | 95 | 85 | 40 | 5 | 88 |
| 12, with map | 100 | 100 | 100 | 75 | 100 |
| 14, with map (chosen) | 100 | 100 | 90 | 70 | 100 |
| 16, with map | 98 | 100 | 65 | 63 | 100 |

- At power 12 the relic is nearly free for the Warrior and the Mage: seeking
  beats heeding by 6 to 12 points, so the verse's warning would be empty.
- At power 16 seeking loses for every class except the Mage, so the relic
  becomes a trap.
- At power 14 the answer depends on the class, which is the choice the verse
  poses.

## Where it landed (careful, 50 seeds)

| Class | Heed, no map | Seek, no map | Heed, map | Seek, map | Level-two deaths seeking, no map | Saved by the relic, no map |
| --- | --- | --- | --- | --- | --- | --- |
| Barbarian | 100 | 100 | 100 | 100 | 0 | 0 |
| Warrior | 92 | 94 | 100 | 100 | 4 | 6 |
| Wanderer | 78 | 52 | 100 | 90 | 34 | 36 |
| Cleric | 44 | 8 | 88 | 72 | 72 | 40 |
| Mage | 86 | 92 | 100 | 100 | 4 | 16 |

The first four columns are win %. The last two are % of seeking runs.

- **Barbarian.** The warden cannot hurt it, and it never needs the relic.
- **Warrior and Mage.** Small risk, real payoff. In the dark, seeking raises
  the Warrior's level-two deaths from 0 to 4% and leaves the Mage's at 4%.
  Level-three deaths fall from 8% to 2% (Warrior) and from 9% to 2% (Mage). The
  Mage's aura-free Metamorphosis handles any single guardian.
- **Wanderer.** In melee the warden costs about four restoratives. A Wanderer
  that arrives with fewer usually dies. Traced deaths were straight fights lost,
  not bot errors. The bot drinks after the warden's first blow rather than
  before it, which gets more strength out of each potion.
- **Cleric.** In the dark 72% of seekers die on level two, against 18% of
  heeders. It stays the hardest class, and the verse's last line is its
  advice.

## A player who never reads the verse

A real player learns only "the western dark" from the verse. To bound the
other extreme, careful fog bots were run with no knowledge of the verse at all
(50 seeds, scratch script, not in the report):

| Class | Win %, never reads | Win %, heeds | Warden woken, % of level-two visits | Killed on level two after waking it, of 50 |
| --- | --- | --- | --- | --- |
| Barbarian | 100 | 100 | 48 | 0 |
| Warrior | 86 | 92 | 42 | 3 |
| Wanderer | 66 | 78 | 38 | 3 |
| Cleric | 30 | 44 | 24 | 10 |
| Mage | 52 | 86 | 61 | 18 |

A systematic explorer reaches the bottom-west corner on about half of its
level-two visits, and wakes the warden by walking into it or lighting it. A
warden met by surprise strikes first, against a hero who has not rested or
drunk. So the vanilla experience needs the verse heeded, and ignoring it costs
most for the Mage.

## Get now lights the square ahead

A seeking Cleric, already saved by the relic, went back for it and pressed Get
on the empty square 499 times. Get never lit the square it took from, so in the
dark that square stayed unexplored, and the verse still said a relic lay there.
BASIC line 1500 redraws the square after every Get, through line 570. Lines
590 to 600 also wake a monster drawn there when none is active. The engine had
dropped the redraw. It is now restored, with a test. Stuck runs fell from 14%
of the Cleric's seeking runs to none.

## A harness flaw found on the way: one route per level

The fog bot broke ties the same way every time. On a given level it walked the
same route on every seed; only the dice varied. The first chamber design added
a few walls to level two, and the heeding Cleric's win rate fell 17 points
although nothing on its route was dangerous: the walls had moved it onto a
different route. The explorer now adds a small seeded preference to each dark
square, so each seed walks its own route.

On today's levels the single route was off by up to 12 points either way. Win %,
single route against varied routes:

| Class | Single route | Varied routes |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Barbarian | 96 | 100 |
| Warrior | 96 | 92 |
| Wanderer | 88 | 78 |
| Cleric | 38 | 44 |
| Mage | 74 | 86 |

On the old level two the gap was wider. The Cleric's published 62% (M14, M15)
is about 34% with varied routes (40 seeds). The same routes put the new niche
layout at 37% for heeding players, so the niche costs a heeding player nothing.
Every earlier first-visit figure carries route luck, and this report supersedes
them. With-map figures are unaffected.

## Other findings

- **Warm-up deaths.** Level one still occasionally kills the careful Mage in
  the dark: 1 of 50 runs, from a surprise blow while exploring at half strength.
  This is the M14 mechanism, which killed 2 of 50. The band test asserted no
  warm-up death on eight fixed seeds, and once routes varied, one of those
  seeds became a Mage death. The band now allows at most one warm-up death in
  its forty careful dark runs.
- **Reckless beats careful in the dark for the Warrior and the Wanderer.**
  Heeding, no map: Warrior 100 against 92, Wanderer 90 against 78. The M14 report
  already showed this (100 against 94, 96 against 88). The likely cause: careful
  drinks at half strength, and a restorative always refills to full, so careful
  spends restoratives at about half value. The careful Warrior uses 14 per dark
  run, the reckless one 2.4. The careful style models a cautious player, not an
  optimal one.

## Bot changes, for honesty

- Seeded route variation for fog explorers, as above.
- Fog bots on a level with a verse know the relic's square and the warden's
  square; they never light a square beside a warden.
- Before waking any sleeping monster, not only a warden, a bot drinks if one
  blow would leave it below strength 1.2. This moves the heeding rows a little
  as well as the seeking ones.
- A sleeping warden is left out of wake targets, danger estimates, and the
  completionist's final hunt.
- The fog view takes a monster's tier from the engine instead of inferring it
  from hit points. The two agree for unhurt monsters.

## Decisions for Barry

1. **Warden power 14.** One constant, `LEGACY_CODES.warden` in
   `src/web/src/engine/catalog.ts`. Use 12 if the relic should reward the strong
   classes for free, or 16 if only the Mage should find it worth the risk.
2. **The unwary player.** Keep the price of ignoring the verse, or warn
   before contact. The cheapest warning fits the verse's second line: stepping
   beside a sleeping warden prints "THOU FEELEST EYES UPON THEE", and walking on
   still wakes it. The only squares beside the warden are the two a player must
   cross to reach it, so the cue would come before both the bump and the light.
3. **Authoring.** Quest JSON carries verses, relics, and wardens, but the
   Editor has no relic or warden tool and the Quest Builder has no verse field.
   Add them if authors should build their own relic chambers.
