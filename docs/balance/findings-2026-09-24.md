## What the harness said about resting

Resting as the original had it, free and unbounded, overshot badly: nearly
every class won nearly every run with or without the map, and the fighters
stopped using potions at all. In the original the only limit on resting was
real-world patience, which a turn-based game removes. So resting is bounded:
it restores strength up to a ceiling, and only potions and healing make a hero
whole. The ceiling was swept with the final bot, careful players in the dark,
win %:

| Ceiling | Barbarian | Warrior | Wanderer | Cleric | Mage |
| --- | --- | --- | --- | --- | --- |
| One half | 100 | 97 | 77 | 20 | 0 |
| Three fifths | 97 | 90 | 83 | 53 | 23 |
| Two thirds, chosen | 100 | 94 | 88 | 62 | 86 |
| Four fifths | 100 | 100 | 87 | 73 | 100 |

The two-thirds row is from the 50-seed report; the others used 30 seeds. The
Mage decides it. With a base strength of 4, each step down the ceiling costs it
nearly a whole monster blow of margin: at three-fifths the careful Mage dies on
level one in 17% of dark runs, and at one half in 73%. At two-thirds that falls
to 4%, the lowest ceiling tried that keeps level one close to a warm-up for
every class. Four-fifths makes the fighters and the Mage untouchable.

## Rules changed

- **Rest** (the `.` key) idles until strength reaches two-thirds of base or a
  monster stands beside a hero off a safe square. No decay while resting; the
  active monster acts every idle turn, as in the archival loop.
- **Traps** hold a hero until they are rested, then a luck roll frees them. The
  BASIC's 80% threshold was out of resting's reach, which left a trapped hero
  without potions pressing Rest about 160 times.
- **The final exit** holds the hero while the Idol remains: "YOU NEED THE IDOL".
  The adventure's abandoned outcome is gone.
- **Reach**: the hero strikes only a monster within one square, as monsters do.
  Before this, the engine let a hero kill an approaching monster from across
  the room, or from a safe square, without being touched. The spec already
  said "in range". The bot only ever attacked adjacent monsters, so this closes
  a human exploit without moving any harness number.

## Content changed: the Idol Vault

- The tier-two monster two squares from the entrance is now a vase: one fewer
  monster, and a potion by the entrance for a hero who arrives depleted.
- The tier-two on the central corridor and the tier-two in the lower-left room
  are gone. The vault's guard and two tier-one monsters remain, so the final
  level is quiet, and the danger is finding the vault and beating its guard.

Clues beyond the new exit message would need a hint field on quest stages or
new room contents from the expansion wishlist, so they were not added.

Deaths now fall almost entirely on level three. In the dark the careful Mage

dies to a single surprise blow.

## Bot changes, for honesty

Traced before trusting: rests every five steps inflated action counts past the
harness cap, and two thresholds fixed as shares of base strength stopped making
sense once resting existed. The bot now rests when genuinely low, drinks out of
combat only when it cannot rest, rests before picking a fight, and never lets
its rest line sit below its danger line.

## Open, for Barry

1. **The Cleric is the hard class.** Its melee needs a potion or two per
   tier-two fight and its aura runs short in the dark. With the map it wins 88%.
2. **Fighting from a safe square is an exploit the harness does not model.** A
   hero on a safe square cannot be struck but can strike, and Sanctuary's
   charges are plentiful. The bot never casts Sanctuary. Either forbid attacking
   from a safe square, or give Sanctuary an aura cost like Superzap's.
3. **Level two is now safe for careful players.** Resting removed the mid-quest
   attrition that used to kill there.
