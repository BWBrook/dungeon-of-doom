# Balance pass findings, 13 September 2026 (M12)

Table: `lost-idol-2026-09-13.md`, 100 seeds per class and bot style.
Regression: `src/web/src/tests/balanceBands.test.ts` pins the careful-bot
bands on seeds 1 to 30.

## Target

Barry's brief: any level could be lethal to any class, but not with high
probability; the melee death rate on level two from the first report was
about right; the three magic classes needed a little more vulnerability.

## Rule changes

Three engine changes, all recorded in `docs/prd/canon_and_errata.md`:

1. Superzap draws one aura per cast, matching Powersurge and Healing.
2. Metamorphosis outcomes are vase, chest, trap, safe place, or a tier-one
   monster. The BASIC range also produced idols, entrances, and exits.
3. Experience gains round to two decimals; five 0.2 gains previously summed
   to 1.9999 and left the exit shut.

## Content changes to The Lost Idol

- Level one: one tier-one monster promoted to tier two beside the chest.
- Level two: the pocket monster and the entrance-side monster promoted to
  tier two; the south-west monster promoted to tier three.
- Level three: the Idol now sits in a walled vault whose single doorway is
  held by a tier-three guard, so it can no longer be walked to; one roaming
  tier-three removed.

## Harness changes

Three bot styles: careful (drinks below half strength, tops up before
fights), reckless (drinks only when the next hit would be fatal),
completionist (careful, but clears the final level before taking the Idol).
The bot conserves aura, keeps one in reserve for the guard, prefers
Metamorphosis to Superzap when melee is not viable, drinks before gambling
when the next hit is fatal, and attacks the monster that actually blocks the
Idol rather than the nearest one. Archetypes were raised to a typical rolled
total of about 45 attribute points.

## Stochastic pass (same day)

Barry asked for a Cleric-specific tweak and, on learning the shield is inert
and the Cleric's runs were deterministic, for more fortune throughout. Two
further rules: monsters miss on a d20 at or under agility, and Superzap
succeeds on a d10 at or under aura plus two. The RNG seed is now hashed,
because small raw seeds gave biased first rolls in every prior run. Tried and
rejected: a d25 dodge (only hurt the Cleric), armour-weighted dodging and one
fewer level-two vase (neither moved the fighters).

## Where it landed (careful bot, death % per level entered)

| Class | Win % | L1 | L2 | L3 | Reckless win % | Completionist win % |
| --- | --- | --- | --- | --- | --- | --- |
| Barbarian | 100 | 0 | 0 | 0 | 96 | 92 |
| Warrior | 100 | 0 | 0 | 0 | 97 | 80 |
| Wanderer | 98 | 0 | 0 | 2 | 96 | 1 |
| Cleric | 75 | 0 | 5 | 21 | 78 | 0 |
| Mage | 92 | 0 | 0 | 8 | 93 | 82 |

Reckless play is slightly safer than careful play for the fighters, because a
potion restores to full whenever it is drunk, so drinking late wastes
nothing. Clearing level three before taking the Idol kills 45 to 100 percent
of runs; the vault guard plus roamers is a deliberate over-reach trap.

## Open items

- **Level one never kills a map-aware bot.** Every class enters at full
  strength with four potions and two vases ahead. A real player without the
  map takes more hits; the bots do not model that. Making level one lethal
  for the bot would need a tier-three pack near the entrance, which felt
  wrong for a first level.
- **Careful fighters are at 100%.** With four potions, four vases, and
  armour 9 they absorb everything a map-aware bot walks into. Their risk now
  lives in reckless play (3 to 4% deaths) and in clearing level three (8 to
  20%). Levers not yet pulled: gear breakage odds, and a level-two design
  that forces a tier-three fight before the vases.
- **The Cleric at 25% deaths** is the most exposed class. The Superzap
  success roll (aura plus two on a d10) is the gradual knob if that is too
  much or too little.
- The shield (slot 15) does nothing in the BASIC or the engine. Whether it
  should join the armour sum is a canon question left open.
